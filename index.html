Kashf Raseed

A personal Android app that copies the text messages (SMS) on its owner's phone into Google Sheets in the owner's own Google Drive, automatically.

How it works

  • The owner allows the app to read SMS and connects their Google Drive.
  • The app creates a "Kashf Raseed" folder in that Drive and one Google Sheet per conversation, with one row per message.
  • New messages are added as they arrive. Messages containing one-time codes are never copied.
  • The app can only see the files it creates. It has no server of its own and sends messages nowhere except the owner's Google Drive.

Read the privacy policy.

Kashf Raseed is a private, non-commercial app made for its owner's own use.
