Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026. This policy covers the Kashf Raseed Android app.

What the app reads

With the user's permission, the app reads the SMS messages stored on the phone (sender or recipient, date and time, whether received or sent, and the message text) and is notified when new messages arrive. Messages that contain one-time passwords or verification codes are detected on the phone and are never copied anywhere.

Where the data goes

The app copies those messages into Google Sheets inside a "Kashf Raseed" folder in the user's own Google Drive, using the Google Drive and Google Sheets APIs. Data travels directly from the phone to Google over an encrypted connection. The app has no server of its own; nothing is sent to the developer or to any other party.

Google account access

The app asks for two Google permissions: access to files the app itself creates in Google Drive (drive.file), and the user's email address, which is shown in the app so the user knows which account is connected. The app cannot see or change any other file in the user's Drive. The user can withdraw access at any time from the app ("Disconnect") or from their Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

What is stored on the phone

The app keeps a list of which messages it has already copied, the IDs of the files it created in Drive, and the connected email address, so that each message is copied only once. This stays on the phone and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Use of Google user data

Kashf Raseed's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the copying described above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties, and not read by any person other than the user.

Deleting the data

The sheets belong to the user and can be deleted from Google Drive at any time. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through github.com/NovaScribe-Tech.
